# The World in One Picture — Sunday 22 November 2026

Entry for make.fun competition `news-image-20261122`: one image that sums up the
world news of 22 November 2026.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` outside the grader).
The image is 1024×1024 PNG, well under the 1 MiB cap, and the copy is
byte-identical on every run. `draw.py` + `content.json` are the generator and
content the PNG was rendered with (Pillow, Noto Sans).

## Stories chosen (all reported on 22 Nov 2026)

1. **COP31 climate talks collapse with no deal** — nearly 200 countries fail to
   agree a declaration in Antalya; the EU and others reject a final text omitting
   any transition away from fossil fuels. (SCMP: "'Did all that we could': Cop31
   climate talks collapse with no deal"; Economic Times, 23 Nov 00:23 IST)
2. **Russian drone hits Kyiv-region flats** — at least 14 people missing after a
   strike on an apartment building, per President Zelenskiy. (Business Recorder,
   World archive 2026-11-22; AP via KIRO 7)
3. **Iran strikes hit Bahrain and Hormuz** — Bahrain says an Iranian attack hit
   an industrial facility; tankers struck in the Strait of Hormuz. (Japan Times,
   22 Nov 2026; Business Recorder)
4. **Drones hit homes and a U.N. building in Khartoum** — pre-dawn strikes on
   Sudan's capital; the army says the drones were launched from Ethiopia.
   (AP / U.N. reporting, 22 Nov 2026; Gulf Times)
5. **Cartel kingpin 'El Chapo Isidro' seized** — Mexican forces capture the FBI
   most-wanted Sinaloa figure in a firefight that leaves 11 dead. (SCMP,
   22 Nov 2026; Economic Times)
6. **Indonesia factory fire · Nigeria boat disaster** — rescuers search for
   dozens missing after a factory fire kills 3; at least 24 die when a boat
   capsizes carrying people to a funeral. (Business Recorder; Dawn, 22 Nov 2026)
